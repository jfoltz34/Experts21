# Brand & Content Creation

## Starting positioning (draft, from docs/08-marketing.md)

> The Tampa daytime destination: a light-filled, Florida-rooted brunch house with a serious pastry and cocktail program, built for celebrations and lazy weekends, and with a reservation actually waiting for you.

Differentiators to express: daytime-only focus, a pastry engine, Florida identity (Gulf, citrus, Cuban/Spanish heritage, local farms), events-first design, an elevated zero-proof list, and guaranteed tables.

## Naming procedure

1. Generate 15-25 candidates across styles: place-based, ingredient-based, evocative, founder or story-based.
2. Screen each one:
   - Easy to say and spell.
   - Not confusable with Tampa competitors (`docs/03-competitors.md`).
   - Domain and social handles available.
   - **Trademark knockout search**: USPTO and Florida Sunbiz (hand off to corporate-counsel).
   - Works on a sign, a menu and an Instagram handle.
3. Shortlist 3-5 with a rationale and the risks for each.

## Voice and tone

Define 3-4 voice traits (e.g., warm, sun-soaked, confident, playful but never cheesy), with "this, not that" examples. Keep copy short and sensory, lead with Florida specifics, and avoid "best brunch in town" clichés.

## Content checklist

- Menu descriptions: under 15 words each. Lead with the hero ingredient, and include the allergen key.
- Website: hero line, reservation call-to-action, menus (as HTML, not PDF-only), events page with inquiry form, gift cards, pastry pre-orders, FAQ (parking, patio, dress, kids, dogs).
- Social captions: hook in the first line, one call-to-action, local hashtags. Avoid overloading.
- Press kit: fact sheet, chef and owner bios, story, high-resolution photos, contact.

## Rules

- No claims that need proof ("best", "only") unless sourced.
- Accessibility: alt text, readable contrast, plain language.
- Use the same name, voice and visual identity on every channel.

## Output

The copy itself, plus a short note explaining how it serves the target segment (see `customer-segmentation`).
