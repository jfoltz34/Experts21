# Capital Raising & Investor Communications

## Capital need

The base-case total is about \$2.66M, with a range of \$1.76M-\$3.95M. The total must include the 6-month reserve and the 15% contingency (the capital gate in 09-risks.md).

## Funding options

| Source | Notes |
|---|---|
| **SBA 7(a)** | Up to \$5M. Typically requires a 10-30% equity injection, a personal guarantee and collateral. 10-year term for business assets, longer if real estate is included. Lenders want a business plan, 3-year projections, personal financial statements, resumes and a signed LOI or lease |
| Equipment financing / leasing | Keeps cash free; the equipment itself is the collateral |
| Landlord tenant-improvement allowance | Effectively landlord capital, repaid through rent. Often \$50-150/sf for strong operators in new developments |
| Investor equity (LLC units) | Common for upscale concepts. Typical structures are a preferred return (8-10%) until capital is returned, then a split (e.g., 70/30 or 80/20 investor/operator), with the operator earning sweat equity |
| Friends & family / community notes | Small checks. Watch securities-law compliance; use counsel |

## Procedure

1. Size the need from the startup budget (`startup-budgeting`), not from a round number.
2. Model debt service against conservative-case EBITDA. The debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) should be ≥1.25x under base and must not fall below 1.0x under conservative, or the reserve must cover the gap.
3. Model investor returns: cash-on-cash by year, payback year, and IRR under all three scenarios. Industry break-even on the investment is 2-3 years.
4. Build the pitch story: the market (growth, affluence, tourism), the gap (daytime destination), the team, the numbers, the risks and their mitigations, and the use of funds.
5. Disclose the key risks honestly (09-risks.md). Lenders and investors discount teams that hide them.

## Investor update template (monthly once open)

Sales vs. plan, covers, check, prime cost %, EBITDA, cash balance and runway, wins, issues, asks.

## Note

Securities offerings and loan covenants need a licensed attorney's review. Flag this to corporate-counsel.
