# Competitor Monitoring

## Core competitor set (from docs/03-competitors.md)

- **Direct, upscale brunch:** Oxford Exchange, On Swann, Ponte Modern American, The Pearl, Boulon Brasserie & Bakery, Predalina, Market at EDITION, Psomi, Counter Culture, Ava, Olivia, Cru Cellars, Meat Market, Ro, Willa's, Flor Fina, Jackson's Bistro, Miguel's.
- **Across the bay:** Allelo, Noble Crust, The Library.
- **Mid-market anchors:** Another Broken Egg, Keke's, First Watch, Snooze.
- **Changes to watch:**
  - Rome + Fig (closed June 2026).
  - Daily Eats, which became Meeting House.
  - Peddler + Potter Oyster House (Bern's group, planned for fall 2027).
  - Rusty Pelican site redevelopment.

## What to capture per competitor

| Field | How |
|---|---|
| Brunch days and hours | Website, Google |
| Price points | Signature entrée, Benedict, pastry, mimosa, cocktail, coffee (from the current menu) |
| Estimated average check | Entrée + one drink + share of an appetizer/pastry. Label **Estimate** |
| Ratings and review count | Google, Yelp, TripAdvisor (note the date) |
| Reservations | Resy/OpenTable availability for Sat 11:00 and Sun 11:30 for 2 and for 6 people, checked about 3 days ahead |
| Programs | Bottomless drinks? Live music or DJ? Private room? Pastry retail? Zero-proof list? |
| Recognition | MICHELIN (star, Bib Gourmand, recommended), TAMPA Magazine / Creative Loafing awards |
| Status | Open, closed, rebranding, new opening (with source and date) |

## Analysis

1. **Price ladder:** plot signature entrée and cocktail prices to see where an upscale price of about \$44 average check sits.
2. **Program gaps:** which programs nobody offers well. Current hypotheses are daytime-only focus, a pastry engine, a Florida-native identity, events-first design, a strong zero-proof list, and guaranteed tables.
3. **Threats:** new entrants from strong groups (Ponte, Pierola, Bern's, Ciccio), and hotel restaurants.
4. **Real estate signals:** closures can free up second-generation spaces. Flag these to the real-estate advisor.

## Output

A dated competitor table, then three to five implications for concept, price or marketing. Note any data older than 90 days.
