## Recurring calendar

- **DBPR food license renewal:** February 1 each year (Hillsborough is District 3).
- **Sales tax:** monthly. **Reemployment tax:** quarterly. **Business tax receipts:** annually.
- **Hood cleaning, backflow test, fire extinguisher and suppression inspections:** per contract and code.
- **Food manager certificates:** track expiry dates.
- **Minimum wage:** recheck every **September 30** (inflation indexing from 2027).

## Procedure

1. Keep the checklist with a Status for each item (Not started / In progress / Submitted / Approved / Blocked), plus Owner, Due and Notes.
2. Every **Verify** item gets an owner and a target date.
3. Flag anything on the critical path to opening. The alcohol special use and the building permit usually drive the date.

## Output

The updated checklist table, the critical-path items, and next actions.
