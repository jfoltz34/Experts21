# Contract Drafting & Review

This is not legal advice. A licensed Florida attorney must review before signing.

## Review procedure

1. **Summarize:** parties, term, price and payment, scope, key obligations, termination.
2. **Red-flag scan:** use the checklist for the contract type below.
3. **Risk-rate each issue:** High (deal-breaker or uncapped liability), Medium, or Low.
4. **Propose fixes:** suggested language or a negotiating position for each High and Medium issue.
5. **Hand off** business terms (price, rent) to the relevant expert. Lease economics go to `lease-analysis`.

## Red flags by contract type

| Contract | Look for |
|---|---|
| **Lease** | Missing contingencies (alcohol approval, permits, insurance), rent commencement not tied to the certificate of occupancy, a personal guarantee with no burn-off, relocation or redevelopment rights, uncapped CAM/NNN, assignment limits that block a sale, radius restrictions, the operating covenant, the hurricane/casualty clause, landlord work definitions |
| **Construction / GC** | Not a guaranteed maximum price (GMP), weak change-order process, no liquidated damages for delay, retainage, warranty, lien waivers, insurance and indemnity, schedule tied to inspections |
| **Design / architect** | Fee basis, scope (MEP and kitchen included?), ownership of drawings, construction administration hours |
| **Vendor / supply** | Price escalators, minimums, exclusivity, rebates, termination, recall and insurance terms |
| **Equipment lease** | Effective rate, buyout, maintenance responsibility, default terms |
| **Events** | Deposits and cancellation, guaranteed counts, food & beverage minimum, service charge disclosure, damage, alcohol rules, force majeure (hurricanes) |
| **Employment / contractor** | At-will language, misclassification risk, non-solicit (Florida enforces reasonable restrictive covenants), confidentiality, recipe and IP ownership |
| **Music** | ASCAP, BMI, SESAC, GMR coverage for background music and DJs |

## Florida notes

- **Force majeure:** name hurricanes, named storms, mandatory evacuations, and utility outages explicitly.
- **Restrictive covenants:** FS 542.335 enforces reasonable covenants.

## Output

The contract summary, an issues table (clause, issue, risk, proposed fix), and a sign / negotiate / walk recommendation.
