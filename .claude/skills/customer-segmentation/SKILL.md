# Customer Segmentation & Persona Modeling

## Segments (from docs/02-target-customers.md)

Spend figures are per person and are **Estimates**.

| Segment | Core occasion | Spend | Daypart | Reach via |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Young professional groups (25-40) | Group brunch, birthdays, game days, Gasparilla | \$45-70 | Weekend | Instagram/TikTok, Resy/OpenTable, local creators |
| Affluent South Tampa families/couples | After church/sports, date brunch, holidays | \$40-60 | Weekend | Word of mouth, reviews, email, school/charity ties |
| Visitors and hotel guests | "Best brunch in Tampa", conventions, cruises | \$50-75 | Weekend + weekday | Google, TripAdvisor, concierges |
| Celebrations and private events | Showers, bachelorettes, graduations, corporate | \$55-90 + minimums | Pre-booked, any day | Website, planners, The Knot, HR outreach |
| Weekday fill: business, remote workers, wellness/GLP-1 users | Power lunch, coffee + pastry, lighter plates | \$20-40 | Weekday | Office outreach, house accounts, pastry counter |

## Must-haves for any segment

Reservations, a cocktail program **and** a strong zero-proof list, shareable "moment" items, dietary range (gluten-free, vegan, high-protein, smaller portions), a cooled covered patio, a parking or valet answer, group-friendly tables, and generous hospitality touches that protect perceived value.

## Procedure

1. Name the segment(s) the decision targets. Avoid "everyone".
2. State the job-to-be-done, the trigger, the decision-maker (often one organizer for a group) and the price sensitivity.
3. Map to dayparts and seasons:
   - Peak: January-April, Easter, Mother's Day, graduations.
   - Trough: June-September.
4. Check the weekday problem. Weekday breakfast is structurally weak in 2026 (the Daily Eats closure cited GLP-1 drugs and breakfast skipping), so weekday plans must rely on non-breakfast hooks.
5. Define success metrics per segment: covers, check, repeat rate, and event bookings.

## Output

A persona card (who, occasion, needs, spend, channel, KPI) or a segment-by-option matrix showing fit.
