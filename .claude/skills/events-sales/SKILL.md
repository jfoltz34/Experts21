# Events Sales & Package Design

## Why events matter

Events are pre-booked, high-margin revenue that fills weekdays and off-peak times. The plan assumes events of \$60K / \$150K / \$250K per year (conservative / base / strong). Typical spend is \$55-90 per person plus minimums.

## Package architecture (Estimates)

| Package | Format | Price guide |
|---|---|---|
| Brunch Social | Family-style brunch + coffee/juice | \$55-65 pp |
| Celebration | Family-style + mimosa/bellini carafes + pastry board + champagne toast | \$75-90 pp |
| Corporate Breakfast | Plated or buffet, coffee service, AV, weekday 7-10am | \$35-50 pp |
| Buyout | Semi-private room or full venue, food & beverage minimum by day and season | Weekday \$2-4K, weekend room \$4-8K (**Estimate**) |

Add-ons: mimosa tower, a decor allowance, custom menus, cake-cutting fee, and pastry favors to go.

## Pricing rules

- Set food & beverage minimums so a buyout matches or beats the displaced à la carte sales for that slot (check with fpa-analyst).
- Add a service charge (e.g., 20-22%), clearly disclosed. Service charges are revenue, not tips (see `wage-labor-compliance`).
- Beverage packages must keep the restaurant's food share ≥51% for SRX. Bundle food.
- Weekday and summer pricing should be lower to drive volume. Peak dates (Mother's Day, Easter, graduations, December) need premium minimums or no buyouts.

## Sales pipeline

Inquiry (web form, phone, Instagram) → respond within 4 business hours → proposal within 24 hours → deposit (25-50%) and signed agreement (see `contract-review`) → final count 7 days out → event → thank-you note, review request and rebooking.

KPIs: inquiry-to-booking conversion (target ≥30%), average event value, lead source, and event revenue as a % of sales.

## Lead sources

Wedding and shower planners, The Knot/WeddingWire, University of Tampa and USF graduations, corporate offices (Westshore and downtown; hospitals like Tampa General and BayCare), hotel concierges, and Instagram.

## Output

A package menu with prices, a minimum-spend table, a proposal template, and a pipeline report.
