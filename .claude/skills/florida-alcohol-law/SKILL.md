# Florida Alcohol Law & SRX Ratio Monitoring

This is not legal advice. A Florida alcoholic-beverage attorney must confirm before anyone acts.

## License options (from docs/06-permits-and-licenses.md)

| License | Allows | Requirements | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2COP | Beer + wine on premises | No quota | Low annual fee (**Verify** the Hillsborough tier) |
| **4COP-SRX** (recommended) | Beer, wine, liquor on premises | **≥2,500 sq ft service area**, equipped to serve **≥150 guests at once**, **≥51% of gross food & beverage revenue from food + non-alcoholic beverages**; revenue audits; not transferable | ≈\$1,820/yr |
| Quota 4COP | Beer, wine, liquor; no food requirement; transferable | Buy on the secondary market | ≈\$1,820/yr + **≈\$210K-\$250K** purchase (Hillsborough) |

## SRX ratio monitoring

- **Ratio** = (food + non-alcoholic beverage sales) ÷ (total food & beverage sales). It must stay **≥51%**. The target buffer is ≥60%.
- Report it monthly from POS category sales. Alert if it falls below 58% on a trailing-3-month basis.
- Common causes of drift: bottomless programs, a bar-heavy events package, falling food prices, or late-morning "drinks only" tables.
- Corrective levers: bundle food in drink packages, raise food attachment, and price carafes as part of brunch sets.

## Other rules to apply

- **Responsible vendor program (FS 561.705):** voluntary, but it gives administrative protection. Train all servers and bartenders, and keep records.
- **Dram shop (FS 768.125):** liability is generally limited to serving minors, or knowingly serving a habitual drunkard. Still, over-service invites suits. Insure accordingly.
- **Minors:** check ID. Follow the rules for under-18 employees handling alcohol (**Verify** the specifics).
- **Hours of sale:** set locally. Confirm the **City of Tampa Sunday morning start time** (**Verify**, Tampa Code Ch. 3). It is critical for brunch.
- **Patio:** confirm that outdoor seating is inside the licensed premises and whether it counts toward the 150 seats (**Verify**).
- **Bottomless/unlimited drinks:** confirm the current ABT position before marketing it (**Verify**).

## Output

The license recommendation and conditions, ratio math, and a list of Verify items with owners.
