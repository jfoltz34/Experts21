# Food Safety & HACCP

## Florida requirements (from docs/06-permits-and-licenses.md)

- Restaurant food licensing and inspection is done by **DBPR Division of Hotels & Restaurants**, not the county health department.
- **Certified Food Protection Managers** (FS 509.039): certify the GM, chefs and all shift managers, so that one is on duty every shift.
- **Food-handler training** (FS 509.049) for every food employee within 60 days of hire, through a DBPR-approved provider. Keep the records on site.
- An opening inspection must be passed before opening. Then routine unannounced inspections.

## High-risk brunch hazards and controls

| Hazard | Control |
|---|---|
| Eggs (runny or poached, Benedicts) | Use pasteurized shell eggs or liquid eggs for undercooked dishes, or post the consumer advisory. Hold shell eggs cold (≤41°F / 5°C). No pooling of raw eggs for undercooked service |
| Hollandaise / emulsions | Make in small batches with pasteurized eggs. Hold at a safe temperature, or use time-as-control with discard times logged (**Verify** current Food Code time limits). Never top up an old batch |
| Raw seafood / oysters | Approved sources and shellfish tags kept for 90 days. Cold holding. Consumer advisory |
| Dairy, cut fruit, juices | Cold holding ≤41°F. Date-mark ready-to-eat foods (7-day rule) |
| Pastry (custards, creams) | Cooling logs (135→70°F within 2 hours, 70→41°F within 4 more hours). Cold holding |
| Allergens | Allergen matrix for every item. POS allergy flag. Dedicated prep tools. Manager verifies the plate |
| Cross-contamination | Color-coded boards. Raw protein separated from pastry and garde-manger |

## Daily and weekly controls

Temperature logs (receiving, cold holding, hot holding, cooling), sanitizer concentration checks, handwashing stations stocked, date labels, pest-control service log, and an employee illness policy (report and exclude symptomatic staff).

## Inspection readiness

Run a monthly mock inspection using the DBPR inspection form categories. Fix critical findings the same day. Keep certificates, training records and logs organized in one binder or a digital system.

## Output

A HACCP-style plan per hazardous item, the SOP list, training and certification tracker, and the mock-inspection checklist.
