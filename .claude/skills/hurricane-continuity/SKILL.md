# Hurricane & Business Continuity Planning

## Context (from docs/09-risks.md)

- Hurricanes Helene and Milton (2024) shut many Tampa Bay restaurants. Florida restaurant sales fell 17.1% year over year in Milton's week.
- Some restaurants were still citing recovery costs in 2026.
- Season: **June 1 - November 30**, with the peak from August to October.

## Before the season (by May 31)

- Inspect the roof. Fit shutters or panels for glass and the patio. Secure or remove patio furniture.
- Generator hookup or transfer switch for the walk-ins and critical circuits. Fuel contract.
- Insurance review: named-storm deductible, flood, and business interruption with civil-authority and utility-outage cover.
- Document everything: photograph and video the premises and inventory for claims.
- Staff contact tree (SMS app), emergency roles, a pay policy during closures, and an evacuation-zone list for staff.
- Vendor plans: supplier recovery, ice and dry-ice sources, a restoration contractor on retainer.

## Storm timeline

| When | Actions |
|---|---|
| 5 days out (cone includes Tampa) | Cut orders, run down perishables, confirm staffing, back up data, notify event clients |
| 72 hours | Decide whether to close. Communicate to staff and guests (Google hours, social, reservation platform). Move or cancel events |
| 48 hours | Install shutters, secure the patio, move equipment off the floor in flood-prone sites, sandbags or flood barriers |
| 24 hours | Final shutdown: gas off, electrical to non-critical circuits off, refrigeration set coldest with temperature monitors, cash and records secured |
| After the storm | Safety check (never enter flooded or electrically compromised areas), photo documentation, insurer notice, food-safety triage (discard anything above 41°F for 4+ hours), DBPR reopening requirements if power or water were lost (**Verify**) |

## Reopening and community

Reopen with a limited menu if supply is constrained. Restaurants often see a demand rebound after storms (displaced residents). Consider community meals or partnerships.

## Cash reserve sizing

Reserve ≥ the named-storm deductible + 2-4 weeks of fixed costs (rent, salaried payroll, insurance) + the cost of replacing spoiled inventory. Coordinate with fpa-analyst.

## Output

A continuity plan document, a checklist by timeline, a roles table, and reserve math.
