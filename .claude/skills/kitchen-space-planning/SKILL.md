# Kitchen & Space Planning

## Program for the reference concept (about 4,000 sq ft + patio)

| Zone | Guideline (Estimate) |
|---|---|
| Dining + bar (service area) | **≥2,500 sq ft** of service area to qualify for SRX; ~15-18 sq ft per seat for upscale |
| Seats | **≥150** to qualify for SRX (target ~160: ~120-130 interior + ~30-40 patio). **Verify** whether patio seats count |
| Seating mix | Mostly 2-tops that combine for 6-12; a few 4-tops and banquettes; bar seats; a **semi-private room** for 20-40 (events) |
| Kitchen + prep + dish | ~30-35% of total area |
| Pastry / bakery + retail counter | Dedicated bench, deck/convection ovens, sheeter if laminating dough; retail case near the entry for weekday traffic |
| Storage | Dry, walk-in cooler, walk-in or reach-in freezer, liquor cage |
| Patio | Covered, with ceiling fans, misters or evaporative cooling, rain protection, and an ADA route |

## Brunch line design

- The bottleneck is the **egg station**: flat-top griddle, French-top or induction burners, a dedicated poaching setup, and a hollandaise holding station with temperature control.
- Other stations: griddle/waffle, garde-manger (salads, raw bar, bowls), fry, pastry pass.
- Design for **~400 covers per day** on weekends with ~2.5 turns. Size the ticket rail and pass for peak (about 11:00-13:00).
- KDS routing by station. Expo position with a clear view of the pass and the dining room.
- Beverage: a service bar near the dining room for carafes and mimosas, an espresso station with its own water and drainage, juice prep, and an ice machine sized for Florida heat.

## Equipment list structure

For each item: item, quantity, specification (size, utilities: gas, electric, water, drain), new vs. used, lead time, cost (**Estimate** until quoted). Coordinate the hood length and Ansul coverage, make-up air, the grease interceptor, and the electrical load with design-construction-manager.

## Checks

- Health code: hand sinks, three-compartment sink, mop sink, prep sinks, finishes. Use DBPR plan review.
- Accessible route and restrooms (ADA).
- Workflow: no cross-traffic between dish return and plating. Separate the pastry area from raw protein.

## Output

A space program table, seat count by zone, station list with capacity rationale, an equipment schedule, and open questions.
