# Lease Analysis & Negotiation

## Market context (from docs/05-operating-costs.md)

- Tampa's average retail asking rent is about \$21.55-28.48/sf/yr, but premium corridors (Hyde Park Village, SoHo, Water Street, Midtown) are an **Estimate** of \$40-65/sf base + \$12-20/sf NNN.
- Tampa Bay retail vacancy was about 3.5% in Q2 2025. A tight market gives landlords leverage.
- The base model assumes about \$70/sf all-in on 4,000 sf, or about \$280K/yr (7.8% of base sales).

## Analysis procedure

1. Normalize each offer to **total occupancy cost** per year and over the initial term: base rent + NNN/CAM + percentage rent (if any) - free rent - tenant-improvement allowance (TIA) amortized.
2. Compute occupancy cost as a **% of sales under all three scenarios**. Require ≤8-10% of **conservative** sales (≈\$2.17M, so ≤ about \$175-217K/yr). Flag if base-case sales are needed to hit 10%.
3. Compare cash needs: deposit, the TIA payment timing (often paid after opening, which requires bridge cash), and rent abatement during build-out.
4. Score the non-economic terms (below).

## Negotiation priorities

1. **Contingencies:** alcohol special use + license, building permits, satisfactory insurance quote, and financing.
2. **Rent commencement** at the earlier of opening or N days after the certificate of occupancy, not a fixed calendar date.
3. **TIA:** \$50-150/sf, paid in progress draws where possible.
4. **Free rent:** 3-6 months after opening.
5. **Term:** 10 years + two 5-year options. Escalations capped at 3%/yr or fixed steps.
6. **Personal guarantee:** limited or "good guy", and burns off after 2-3 years of on-time rent.
7. **CAM/NNN cap** on controllable expenses (e.g., 5%/yr).
8. **Protections:** co-tenancy (new developments), exclusive use (no other brunch-focused restaurant), relocation and redevelopment limits, and casualty/hurricane abatement.
9. **Parking or valet rights**, patio rights, signage, and extended hours for evening private events.

## Output

An offer comparison table (economics + key terms), occupancy % by scenario, and the recommended counter-offer points.
