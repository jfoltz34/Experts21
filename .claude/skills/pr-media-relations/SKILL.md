# PR & Media Relations

## Media targets (Tampa Bay)

- **Food press:** Tampa Bay Times (food critic, restaurant news), Creative Loafing Tampa (food and drink, Best of the Bay), TAMPA Magazine (Best Restaurants readers' poll), That's So Tampa, Tampa Bay Business Journal (business angle).
- **Broadcast:** TV morning shows (WFLA, WTSP, FOX 13, Bay News 9). Good for chef demos.
- **Tourism:** Visit Tampa Bay (listings, media familiarization tours, MICHELIN page). Hotel concierge networks.
- **Long-term:** MICHELIN Guide Florida (Tampa has had 3 one-star restaurants since 2026, and Bib Gourmands such as Psomi). Recognition follows consistency, not pitching.

## Pitch angles

The daytime-only destination concept, the chef's story, Florida sourcing (Plant City, Gulf, citrus), the pastry program, a zero-proof program for the GLP-1 / sober-curious era, the design story, community and charity partnerships (e.g., Feeding Tampa Bay), and the jobs created.

## Opening timeline

- **T-4 months:** hire a local hospitality PR firm. Offer an exclusive announcement to one top outlet.
- **T-2 months:** chef and concept features, then a menu reveal.
- **T-1 month:** media preview brunches, staggered. Open the reservation date to the press.
- **Opening week:** ribbon-cutting with the chamber or neighborhood association, plus a charity soft opening.
- **Months 2-6:** follow-up stories (new menu, events, awards entries). Enter TAMPA Magazine and Creative Loafing reader polls.

## Crisis communications

1. Acknowledge quickly with facts.
2. Have one spokesperson.
3. Never speculate.
4. Coordinate with legal and insurance on incidents (food safety, injury, alcohol).
5. Update Google and social media with operational status (e.g., hurricane closures).
6. Do a post-incident review.

## Output

A media list, a pitch email, a press release, a timeline, or a crisis holding statement.
