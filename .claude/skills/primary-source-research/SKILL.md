# Web & Primary-Source Research

## When to research

- The research pack in `docs/` doesn't cover the fact.
- The fact is labeled **Verify**.
- The fact changes often: wages, fees, rents, competitor status, rules.

## Source hierarchy (prefer higher)

1. **Primary or official sources:**
   - Florida DBPR: Hotels & Restaurants, and Alcoholic Beverages & Tobacco (myfloridalicense.com).
   - City of Tampa (tampa.gov): zoning, AB special use, permits.
   - Hillsborough County (hcfl.gov) and its Tax Collector.
   - FL Dept. of Revenue (floridarevenue.com).
   - U.S. DOL, BLS, Census, FEMA flood maps, IRS, SBA.
   - Visit Tampa Bay.
   - The MICHELIN Guide.
   - Restaurants' own websites and menus.
2. **Reputable secondary sources:** Tampa Bay Times, Creative Loafing Tampa, TAMPA Magazine, Tampa Bay Business Journal, Cushman & Wakefield / CBRE reports, National Restaurant Association.
3. **Tertiary sources** (use only for leads, then confirm): vendor blogs, listicles, social media, forums.

## Procedure

1. Search with specific terms: the agency name, the statute number, "Hillsborough", "Tampa", and the year.
2. Open the actual page. Don't rely on search snippets for numbers.
3. Capture the exact figure, its effective date, and the URL.
4. If two sources conflict, prefer the primary source and note the conflict.
5. If you can't confirm a fact, keep it labeled **Verify** and say what you tried.

## Recording what you found

Report every new fact as:
`<fact> - <source name>, <URL>, retrieved <YYYY-MM-DD>`

If the fact should update the research pack, say which `docs/` file and section it belongs in. Don't edit the docs unless asked.

## Privacy

Never put the consultant's or the project's private details into search queries or requests to outside services.
