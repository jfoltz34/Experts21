# Procurement & Vendor Management

## Vendor map

| Category | Typical sources (Tampa) |
|---|---|
| Broadline food | National broadliners (Sysco, US Foods, PFG) plus a specialty distributor |
| Produce | Local produce distributors. Florida farms: Plant City strawberries (Dec-Apr), citrus, tomatoes, greens |
| Seafood | Gulf seafood suppliers (grouper, shrimp, snapper, oysters), with shellfish tags |
| Dairy, eggs | Pasteurized-egg supplier (see `food-safety-haccp`). Local dairy where available |
| Bakery inputs | European-style butter and flour for laminated pastry |
| Coffee | Local Tampa roaster (brand story + equipment programs) |
| Alcohol | Florida's three-tier system: buy only from licensed distributors |
| Equipment / smallwares | Restaurant supply dealers. Used-equipment dealers for savings |
| Services | Linen, hood cleaning, pest control, grease trap, waste/recycling, HVAC/refrigeration repair |

## Procedure

1. **Spec first:** write product specifications (grade, size, pack) from the recipe cards (`menu-engineering`).
2. **Bid:** get 2-3 quotes per category. Compare landed cost (delivery fees, minimums, payment terms, credit policy).
3. **Negotiate:** price locks or caps on the top 20 items (about 80% of spend), rebates and volume tiers, delivery days matched to the brunch prep schedule (Thursday-Friday deliveries for the weekend), net-30 terms.
4. **Order guides and par levels:** set pars by day of week (the weekend needs about 2× weekday). Order against pars minus on-hand.
5. **Inventory:** count weekly (food and beverage separately). Run actual-vs-theoretical cost of goods variance (target under 2%), FIFO, and waste logs.
6. **Local sourcing:** feature it on the menu for brand value. Watch seasonality and price swings, and keep a backup supplier for every critical item.
7. **Hurricane prep:** cut inventory ahead of named storms and confirm supplier recovery plans (see `hurricane-continuity`).

## Output

A vendor shortlist with terms, an order guide and par sheet structure, cost-of-goods control KPIs, and savings opportunities.
