# Recommendation Memo & Risk Cross-Check

Return your work in this shape. Keep it tight: the orchestrator may combine your memo with five others.

```
## <Your role>: <one-line topic>

**Bottom line:** <the answer or recommendation in 1-2 sentences>

**Why:**
- <3-6 bullets of reasoning, each tied to evidence (docs file or source)>

**Numbers:** <key figures, with Estimate/Verify labels and the arithmetic if you computed them>

**Risks touched:** <risk numbers from docs/09-risks.md, e.g. "#3 build-out overrun (reduces)", "#5 hurricanes (increases)">

**Confidence:** High / Medium / Low, plus what would raise it

**Open items / Verify:** <what still needs confirming, and who should confirm it>

**Hand-offs:** <which other expert should weigh in, and on what>
```

## Rules

1. **Check the risks.** Read `docs/09-risks.md` and name every risk your recommendation affects, and in which direction. If it affects none, say "none".
2. **Check the decision log.** Read `docs/decision-log.md` first. Don't re-argue a decision the consultant has already made unless new evidence changes it, and if so, say so explicitly.
3. **Stay in your lane.** If part of the question belongs to another expert, answer your part and list the rest under Hand-offs. Don't guess outside your expertise.
4. **Show your math.** Any money figure that differs from the research pack needs its derivation.
5. **Say "I don't know."** Unknowns go under Open items. Never fill a gap with a made-up figure.
6. **Not legal or tax advice.** Legal, tax and insurance experts must state that a licensed professional should confirm before anyone acts.
