# Recruiting, Training & Org Design

## Org and pay (from docs/07-staffing.md, Estimates)

- **Leadership:** GM (\$75-100K + bonus), Executive Chef (\$80-115K + bonus), AGMs (\$55-70K), Sous Chef (\$55-68K), Pastry Chef (\$50-65K), Events Coordinator (\$45-60K).
- **Hourly:**
  - Line cooks \$18-23, prep \$16-18, dish \$15-17.
  - Servers and bartenders at \$11.98 + tips (servers \$25-40/hr all-in on weekends).
  - Hosts \$15-17.
- **BLS Tampa medians (May 2023, now outdated by the \$15 floor):** restaurant cooks \$17.23, chefs \$26.53, food service managers \$30.83.

## Hiring timeline (backward from opening)

| When | Hire |
|---|---|
| T-4 to T-3 months | GM, Executive Chef (they shape the concept and the team) |
| T-2 months | AGMs, Sous Chef, Pastry Chef, Bar lead, Events Coordinator |
| T-6 to T-4 weeks | All hourly staff (hiring fair, about 1.3× the needed headcount to allow for drop-off) |
| T-3 to T-1 weeks | Training, then friends-and-family mock services |

## Sources

Culinary programs at Hillsborough Community College and similar, Culinary Agents, Poached, Indeed, referrals (with bonuses), staff from closed restaurants (e.g., the 2026 Rome + Fig closure), and University of Tampa and USF students for weekend shifts.

## Training program (pre-opening, 2-3 weeks)

Culture and hospitality standards, menu and allergen training with tastings, wine/cocktail/zero-proof training, steps of service (see `hospitality-service-design`), POS and KDS, food safety (certified managers; food-handler training within 60 days), responsible alcohol service, emergency and hurricane procedures, then mock services with friends and family.

## Retention levers

- The **daytime schedule** (no late nights) is the headline recruiting pitch.
- A clear ladder: runner → server → captain → manager; prep → line → sous.
- Predictable weekend rotations, shift meals, a quarterly bonus pool tied to reviews and sales, health benefits for full-timers, and recognition.
- Target hourly turnover under 50% (the industry runs 70%+). Run stay interviews at 30, 90 and 180 days.

## Output

The org chart, job descriptions, pay bands, a hiring plan with timeline and sources, the training curriculum, and retention KPIs.
