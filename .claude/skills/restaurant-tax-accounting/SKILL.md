# Tax & Accounting

## Florida and Tampa specifics

- **Sales tax:** 7.5% in Hillsborough County (6% state + 1.5% county surtax).
  - Register with the FL Dept. of Revenue **before the first sale**. File monthly at this volume.
  - Mandatory service charges are generally taxable. Voluntary tips are not. Confirm current FL DOR guidance (**Verify**).
- **No Florida personal income tax.** The Florida corporate income tax applies to C-corps. Pass-through LLC income flows to the owners' federal returns.
- **Reemployment (unemployment) tax:** a Florida employer account through FL DOR. File quarterly.
- **Local business tax:** annual City of Tampa Business Tax Receipt and Hillsborough County Local Business Tax Receipt.
- **Tangible personal property tax:** file an annual DR-405 with the Hillsborough Property Appraiser for furniture, fixtures and equipment (**Verify** the exemption threshold).

## Payroll and tips

- Employees must report tips. The employer withholds and pays FICA on reported tips.
- **FICA tip credit (IRC §45B):** a federal credit for employer Social Security and Medicare paid on tips above the federal minimum-wage threshold. It is material for a tipped staff of 30+ people. Coordinate with the CPA.
- Use Form 8027 if the restaurant has more than 10 employees on a typical day and tipping is customary.
- Keep tip pools and service charges separate in the books. Service charges are revenue, and the payouts from them are wages.

## Chart of accounts (restaurant, USAR-style)

| Group | Lines |
|---|---|
| Sales | Food, Liquor, Beer, Wine, Non-alcoholic beverages, Retail pastry, Events, Service charges |
| Cost of goods | Mirror each sales category |
| Labor | FOH hourly, BOH hourly, Management salaries, Payroll taxes, Benefits, Workers' comp |
| Operating expenses | Rent, NNN, Utilities, R&M, Marketing, Supplies, Linen, Technology, Card fees, Insurance, G&A |

Category-level cost of goods lets the team monitor food vs. alcohol mix (for the SRX 51% food test) and pour costs.

## Month-end close checklist

1. Bank and card reconciliations.
2. Inventory count, food and beverage separately.
3. Accrue payroll and invoices.
4. Sales tax liability tie-out.
5. Tip and service-charge tie-out.
6. P&L vs. budget, with prime cost %.
7. Food vs. alcohol sales ratio for SRX compliance.

## Note

Tax treatment must be confirmed by a licensed CPA. Treat this skill as a checklist, not tax advice.
