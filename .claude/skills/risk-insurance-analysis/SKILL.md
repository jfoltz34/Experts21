# Risk Assessment & Insurance Analysis

## Risk register (from docs/09-risks.md)

1. Weak weekday demand (Critical)
2. Soft consumer spending (Critical)
3. Build-out overrun / delays (Critical)
4. Labor cost and talent (High)
5. Hurricanes, flood and insurance (High)
6. Alcohol licensing / zoning (High)
7. Competition (High)
8. Seasonality (Medium)
9. Lease and real estate (Medium)
10. Liability (Medium)
11. Opening reputation (Medium)

## Scoring procedure

1. **Likelihood** (1-5) × **Impact** (1-5) = score.
   - 15+ Critical, 10-14 High, 5-9 Medium, under 5 Low.
2. For each risk, record: trigger or early-warning indicator, mitigation, owner (expert), residual score, and the date reviewed.
3. When a proposal arrives, state how it moves each affected risk, and update the register in your output. Don't edit docs unless asked.

## Insurance program (Estimate premiums; get broker quotes)

| Coverage | Notes |
|---|---|
| Commercial property (building improvements, equipment, inventory) | Florida policies carry a separate **named-storm deductible**, typically a % of insured value. Model the out-of-pocket cost for a storm |
| Flood | Usually excluded from property. Price NFIP plus private excess, especially in South Tampa, Davis Islands, Harbour Island and the Channel District |
| Business interruption / extra expense | Include civil-authority closure, utility service interruption, and spoilage |
| General liability | \$1M / \$2M typical |
| **Liquor liability** | Essential with a full bar. Bottomless programs raise the rate and the exposure |
| Workers' compensation | Required with 4+ employees in Florida |
| EPLI | Employment claims at about 60 employees |
| Umbrella | \$2-5M over GL, liquor and auto |
| Cyber | POS and payment data |

The realistic total for this concept is about **\$35-75K/yr** (an **Estimate**). Published small-business quotes of \$3.5-7K are far too low.

## Procedure for insurance decisions

Get indications **before** signing a lease (site affects wind and flood), compare 2-3 brokers, check the exclusions and the deductibles, and size the cash reserve to cover the deductible plus 2-4 weeks of closure.

## Output

The updated risk table (score, trend, mitigation, owner), plus an insurance coverage matrix with gaps.
