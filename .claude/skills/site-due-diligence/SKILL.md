# Zoning, Land Use & Site Due Diligence

## Site scorecard

Score each item Pass / Concern / Fail, with evidence.

| Area | Check |
|---|---|
| **Alcohol zoning** | Does the site already hold City of Tampa Alcoholic Beverage Special Use approval for restaurant sales, and can it transfer? If not, is it S-1 (administrative) or S-2 (City Council hearing)? |
| **Distance separations** | Distance to schools, places of worship, residential uses and other alcohol establishments per Tampa Code Ch. 27. Are waivers needed? |
| **Zoning district** | Is a restaurant a permitted use? What are the parking requirements and outdoor seating rules? |
| **SRX fit** | ≥2,500 sq ft of service area and ≥150 seats achievable, and does the patio count? (**Verify**) |
| **Historic district** | Hyde Park, Ybor (Barrio Latino), Seminole Heights: needs review for exterior changes and signs |
| **Flood** | FEMA flood zone and base flood elevation. Would a substantial-improvement rule trigger floodproofing? Elevation of the finished floor. Storm surge exposure (South Tampa, Bayshore, Davis Islands, Harbour Island, Channel District) |
| **Infrastructure** | Existing hood and exhaust path, grease interceptor, gas service, electrical amperage, HVAC tonnage, restrooms, ADA path |
| **Parking and access** | On-site, garage or valet options; rideshare drop-off; visibility; weekend congestion |
| **Insurance** | Get a property, wind and flood premium indication before the LOI (risk-insurance-advisor) |
| **Redevelopment** | Will the landlord or the site be redeveloped during the lease term? |

## Procedure

1. Desk screen: zoning map, the City of Tampa AB approval history, the FEMA flood map, the historic district map, and the existing use.
2. Site visit with the design-construction-manager and foodservice-designer to check the infrastructure.
3. Legal check: the liquor-zoning-attorney confirms the AB path and distance waivers.
4. Insurance indication: risk-insurance-advisor.
5. The verdict feeds the **Site gate** (09-risks.md): AB path confirmed, flood and insurance acceptable, occupancy ≤10% of conservative-case sales.

## Lease protections to require (hand off to lease-analysis)

Contingencies for alcohol approval, building permits and the insurance quote, plus a rent-commencement date tied to the certificate of occupancy.

## Output

A scorecard per site with an overall rating (Go / Conditional / No-go) and the conditions.
