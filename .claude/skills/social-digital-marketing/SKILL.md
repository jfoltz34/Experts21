# Social Media & Digital Marketing

## Channel roles (from docs/08-marketing.md)

| Channel | Role | KPI |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram + TikTok | Discovery for ages 25-40; "moment" dishes, cocktails, design, behind the scenes | Saves, shares, reservation link clicks |
| Google Business Profile | Visitors' top channel ("best brunch Tampa") | Views, direction requests, rating ≥4.6 |
| Resy / OpenTable | Booking + editorial discovery | Reservation share, no-show rate |
| Email / SMS (owned list) | Weekday and summer fill, events, holiday pre-sales | List growth, attributable covers |
| TripAdvisor / Yelp | Visitor trust | Rating, review velocity |

## Pre-opening digital playbook

- **T-6 months:** secure handles, launch a landing page with an email/SMS waitlist.
- **T-3 months:** behind-the-scenes construction and chef content, 3-4 posts per week.
- **T-2 months:** claim Google Business Profile with the opening date; open reservations.
- **T-1 month:** staggered creator previews of 8-12 local food creators each (disclosure required: #ad/#gifted per FTC rules).
- **Opening:** prioritize the waitlist for reservations.

## Always-on rhythm

- Instagram: 4-5 posts per week plus daily stories. TikTok: 3 per week.
- Content pillars: the dishes, the drinks, the people, Florida sourcing, events, behind the scenes.
- Google: weekly posts and photos, answer Q&A, and keep hours accurate (especially holidays and storms).
- Reviews: respond to **all** reviews within 48 hours. Take negatives offline and follow the service-recovery playbook.
- Email/SMS: a monthly newsletter, plus targeted campaigns: birthdays, a summer locals program, holiday pre-sales (Mother's Day, Easter, New Year's Day), and gift cards in Q4.
- Paid: small geo-targeted budgets around launch and holidays. Measure cost per reservation.

## Local calendar hooks

Gasparilla (January), Valentine's, Easter, **Mother's Day** (the biggest day), graduations, Tampa Bay Restaurant Month (June), football season, holidays.

## Output

A content calendar, a campaign brief (goal, audience, message, channel, budget, KPI), or a reputation report.
