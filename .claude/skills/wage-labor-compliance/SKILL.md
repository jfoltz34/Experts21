# Employment Law & Wage Compliance

This is not legal advice. Employment counsel should confirm before anyone acts.

## Current rules (from docs/07-staffing.md)

- **Florida minimum wage: \$15.00/hr** since 9/30/2026. **Tipped cash wage: \$11.98/hr** (maximum tip credit \$3.02). Indexed to inflation every September 30 from 2027.
- A tipped employee must regularly receive more than \$30/month in tips to count as tipped. If tips plus cash wage fall short of \$15.00 in a workweek, the employer pays the difference.
- **Overtime:** 1.5× the regular rate over 40 hours. For tipped staff, overtime is computed on the full minimum wage (\$22.50 minus the tip credit).
- **E-Verify:** required for private employers with **25+ employees** (FL SB 1718, effective July 1, 2023).
- **Workers' compensation:** required with 4+ employees.

## Tip pools and service charges (FLSA)

- Managers and supervisors may **never** keep tips from a pool.
- If the employer takes a tip credit, the pool can include only customarily tipped employees (servers, bartenders, bussers, runners, hosts if tipped). Back-of-house cannot share.
- If **no** tip credit is taken (everyone paid at least \$15.00), back-of-house may share in the pool.
- **Service charges** (e.g., automatic 20% for 8+ guests) are restaurant revenue, not tips. If distributed, they are paid as wages, are taxable, and must be disclosed clearly to guests.

## Minors (16-17-year-old hosts or bussers)

Florida child-labor hour limits apply on school days and during school weeks. Restrictions also apply to serving or handling alcohol (**Verify** the current FS 450 details).

## Compliance checklist

1. Written tip-pool policy, with acknowledgment signed by staff.
2. The POS records cash and credit tips by employee, and payroll tests the minimum-wage make-up every week.
3. Classify salaried managers correctly under the exempt rules (duties test + salary threshold).
4. Required posters (federal + Florida minimum wage), I-9 + E-Verify, and new-hire reporting.
5. Meal and rest breaks: none required by Florida law for adults. Follow the stated policy consistently.

## Output

A compliant pay structure (by role), the risks of alternatives, and items for counsel.
