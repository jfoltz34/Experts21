# 02 — Target Customers

_Research compiled November 2026. Personas are synthesized from the demographic and trend data in [01-market-overview.md](01-market-overview.md). Spend figures are **Estimates**._

## Core insight

Upscale brunch in Tampa is an **occasion purchase**: a celebration, a social ritual, a visitor's "must-do," or a lazy luxury weekend. Guests are paying for the experience (design, cocktails, photos, hospitality, a sense of event) as much as the eggs. Weekend demand is deep. Weekday demand is thin, and in 2026 it is getting thinner (see the Daily Eats closure in [09-risks.md](09-risks.md)). So the primary segments below are weekend-driven, and the secondary segments exist to fill weekdays.

## Primary segments

### 1. Young professional social groups (ages 25–40)
- **Who:** Single or newly partnered professionals in finance, healthcare, tech, law, and the military (MacDill AFB). They live in SoHo, Hyde Park, Channel District, downtown towers, Westshore and Seminole Heights. Tampa's median age is about 36.
- **Occasion:** Weekend group brunch for 4–8 people, birthdays, "Sunday Funday," pre- or post-game (Bucs, Lightning), Gasparilla.
- **Wants:** Cocktail program (mimosa/bellini flights, espresso martinis, spritzes), a lively vibe or DJ, a photogenic room and plates, reservations or a waitlist app, split checks.
- **Spend (Estimate):** $45–70 per person with 2+ drinks.
- **Reach via:** Instagram, TikTok, Resy/OpenTable, influencer and "That's So Tampa"-style local media.

### 2. Affluent South Tampa families and couples (ages 35–60)
- **Who:** Households earning $200K+ (about 16% of city households, concentrated in Hyde Park, Palma Ceia, Bayshore, Davis Islands, Beach Park, plus Westchase and Carrollwood).
- **Occasion:** After church or kids' sports, date brunch, extended-family celebrations, Mother's Day, Easter.
- **Wants:** Consistent quality, comfortable and refined service, parking, kids who are tolerated but not catered to, a good coffee and wine program, a quiet zone away from a party table.
- **Spend (Estimate):** $40–60 per adult; high lifetime value and frequency.
- **Reach via:** Word of mouth, TAMPA Magazine / Tampa Bay Times reviews, Google, email list, local school and charity sponsorships.

### 3. Visitors and hotel guests
- **Who:** Leisure travelers, convention attendees, cruise passengers before or after sailing, wedding parties, UT/USF parents. Downtown visitor median HH income is about $92K and rising (Placer.ai).
- **Occasion:** "Best brunch in Tampa" search, a weekend getaway, a convention Sunday.
- **Wants:** Discoverability (Google, TripAdvisor, concierge), a "Florida" sense of place (patio, waterfront or river views, Gulf seafood, citrus, Cuban-heritage nods to Ybor), easy booking.
- **Spend (Estimate):** $50–75 per person.
- **Reach via:** Hotel concierges, Visit Tampa Bay listings, Google Business Profile, TripAdvisor, OpenTable.

### 4. Celebrations and private events
- **Who:** Bachelorette parties, bridal and baby showers, milestone birthdays, graduation brunches (UT, USF), corporate team brunches.
- **Occasion:** Pre-booked group of 10–40 people.
- **Wants:** A semi-private room or buyout, prix-fixe or family-style menus, packages (champagne toast, mimosa tower), decor allowances.
- **Spend (Estimate):** $55–90 per person, with minimums. High margin and highly predictable.
- **Reach via:** Website events page, wedding planners, The Knot, Instagram, corporate HR and EA outreach.

## Secondary segments (weekday fill)

| Segment | Use case | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| Business breakfast / lunch (Westshore, downtown) | Client meetings, 45–60 min | Fast "power lunch" menu, Wi-Fi, quiet tables, house account |
| Remote workers and "ladies who lunch" | Late-morning coffee and pastry, a long lunch | Pastry and coffee bar, grab-and-go retail case, rosé by the glass |
| Wellness-minded / GLP-1 users | Smaller, protein-forward plates | Half portions, protein bowls, zero-proof cocktails, functional drinks |
| Tourists on weekdays | Same as above | Concierge partnerships |

## Customer requirements checklist (for concept and menu agents)

1. **Reservations** for weekend peaks (Resy or OpenTable), plus a waitlist for walk-ins.
2. **A cocktail program** as a signature, but also a strong **zero-proof** list (sober-curious and GLP-1 trends).
3. **Shareable "moment" items** (towers, tableside elements, signature pastry) for social media.
4. **Dietary range:** gluten-free, vegetarian/vegan, high-protein, smaller formats.
5. **Covered and air-conditioned outdoor seating.** Tampa heat and summer rain make a misted/fanned covered patio essential, not optional.
6. **Parking or valet**, a real issue in SoHo and Hyde Park. Water Street has garages.
7. **Group-friendly** layout: tables that combine for 6–12, plus a semi-private room.
8. **Perceived value:** at upscale price points diners still judge "worth it," given the 2026 consumer cutbacks. Generous hospitality touches (welcome bite, coffee refills) protect value perception.

## Seasonality of demand

- **Peak:** January–April (snowbirds, Gasparilla, spring break, pleasant weather, MLB spring training nearby), plus graduation season, Mother's Day and Easter.
- **Shoulder:** October–December (holidays, football season).
- **Trough:** June–September (heat, rain, hurricane season, fewer tourists). Tampa Bay Restaurant Month (June) and summer promotions help.
