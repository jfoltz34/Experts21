## Revenue model (Estimate)

Assumes about 160 seats, open 8am–3pm daily, a weekend-heavy mix, and an average check per guest including beverages.

| Driver | Conservative | Base | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sat + Sun covers (combined) | 600 | 800 | 1,000 |
| Weekday covers (Mon–Fri combined) | 450 | 650 | 900 |
| Covers/week | 1,050 | 1,450 | 1,900 |
| Avg check (food + bev, pre-tax, pre-tip) | $38 | $44 | $50 |
| Weekly sales | $39,900 | $63,800 | $95,000 |
| Private events / catering (annual) | $60,000 | $150,000 | $250,000 |
| Retail pastry/coffee (annual) | $40,000 | $100,000 | $180,000 |
| **Annual sales** | **≈$2.17M** | **≈$3.57M** | **≈$5.37M** |

Seat turns: in the base case the weekend averages about 2.5 turns per day on 160 seats; weekdays average about 0.8 turns. **The weekday line carries the most uncertainty** (see the Daily Eats closure in [09-risks.md](09-risks.md)). Seasonality: model summer (Jun–Sep) at about 80–85% of the annual average and Jan–Apr at about 115–120%.

## Labor model (Base case)

See [07-staffing.md](07-staffing.md). Summary: about 50–65 employees (heavily part-time), salaried team of GM, Executive Chef, 2 AGMs/FOH managers, Sous Chef, and optionally a Pastry Chef. Weekend labor is roughly 2× weekday labor.

## Operating levers

1. **Weekend price architecture:** a prix-fixe brunch or "brunch for the table" set menu raises check and simplifies the kitchen.
2. **Beverage attach rate:** target about 1.3 alcoholic drinks per guest on weekends. Mimosa/bellini carafes have lower labor and higher margin than individual cocktails.
3. **Reservation deposits / card holds** for parties of 6+ and holidays reduce no-shows.
4. **Daytime-only hours** save a dinner shift's management, utilities and late-night security, and help with staff recruiting.
5. **Events** for off-peak weekday fill (showers, corporate breakfasts) and, if the lease allows, evening buyouts.
6. **Energy:** high-efficiency HVAC and a shaded patio with ceiling fans. Florida A/C is a top-three controllable cost.
