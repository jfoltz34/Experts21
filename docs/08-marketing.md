# 08 — Marketing Plan

_Research compiled November 2026. Channel recommendations draw on local media and market observations in this research set. Budgets are **Estimates**._

## Positioning statement (draft for concept agents to refine)

> _The Tampa daytime destination: a light-filled, Florida-rooted brunch house with a serious pastry and cocktail program, built for celebrations and lazy weekends, and with a reservation actually waiting for you._

This addresses the gaps identified in [03-competitors.md](03-competitors.md): daytime-only focus, a pastry engine, a Florida identity, event-first design, a strong zero-proof program, and reservation-led service.

## Budget (Estimate)

| Phase | Budget | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-opening (T-4 months → opening) | $40K–$110K | Brand identity, website, photography, PR agency retainer, influencer and media previews, friends & family |
| Year 1 ongoing | 3–4% of sales (~$100K–$140K at base case) | Heavier in months 1–6 |
| Year 2+ | 2–3% of sales | Lean on retention, events and earned media |

## Pre-opening playbook (T-minus)

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| T-6 mo | Lock name, brand identity, and Instagram/TikTok handles. Launch a "coming soon" landing page with an email/SMS waitlist |
| T-4 mo | Hire a local hospitality PR firm. Pitch an exclusive announcement to **Tampa Bay Times**, **Creative Loafing Tampa**, **TAMPA Magazine**, **That's So Tampa**, **Tampa Bay Business Journal** |
| T-3 mo | Behind-the-scenes construction content and chef introduction. Seed hard-hat tours for local food creators |
| T-2 mo | Open Resy/OpenTable and claim a Google Business Profile (with "opening soon" date). Meet hotel concierges (EDITION, JW Marriott, Marriott Water Street, Epicurean, Armature Works area) |
| T-1 mo | Media and influencer preview brunches (stagger them, about 8–12 creators each). Neighborhood association and HOA invites. Charity soft-opening (e.g., benefiting Feeding Tampa Bay) |
| Opening week | Soft opening at limited capacity, then a grand opening weekend. Prioritize reservations for the waitlist list |

## Always-on channels

| Channel | Role | KPI |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram + TikTok** | Primary discovery for the 25–40 segment; showcase "moment" dishes, cocktails, design | Follower growth, saves/shares, reservation link clicks |
| **Google Business Profile** | #1 channel for visitors ("best brunch Tampa"); photos, menu, Q&A, posts | Profile views, direction requests, rating ≥4.6 |
| **Resy / OpenTable** | Booking plus discovery (editorial lists, "New & Notable") | Reservation %, no-show rate, cover counts |
| **Email/SMS (owned list)** | Drive weekday and summer visits, events, holiday pre-sales | List size, open/click, attributable covers |
| **TripAdvisor / Yelp** | Visitor trust; respond to all reviews within 48 hrs | Rating, review velocity |
| **Website** | Menus, events and private-dining inquiry form, gift cards, retail pastry pre-orders | Event leads, gift-card sales |
| **Earned media / awards** | Credibility: TAMPA Magazine Best Restaurants (Readers' Choice), CL Best of the Bay, Tampa Bay Times Top 50, MICHELIN Guide (long-term goal) | Placements, award nominations |

## Partnerships

- **Hotels and concierges:** referral cards, a reserved concierge table allocation on weekends, and a staff tasting night.
- **Event planners and wedding vendors:** shower and bachelorette packages listed on The Knot, WeddingWire and planner networks.
- **Corporate:** Westshore and downtown offices (law firms, banks, hospitals: Tampa General, BayCare) for breakfast meetings and team events. Offer house accounts.
- **Local producers:** Plant City strawberries, Florida citrus, Gulf seafood, Tampa coffee roasters, Cigar City / local breweries. These give co-marketing and authenticity.
- **Community:** Junior League of Tampa, school foundations (Plant High, Gorrie, Berkeley Prep), charity brunches.

## Retention and loyalty

- **Guest CRM** through the reservation platform: tag birthdays and anniversaries, track VIPs and dietary notes.
- **Lightweight loyalty:** a "Regulars" program (e.g., 10th visit gets a pastry board; priority booking for holidays).
- **Summer resident program** to fight seasonality (June–September).
- **Recover bad experiences fast:** a manager touch at every table on weekends, and a review-response SLA.

## Measurement dashboard (for analytics agents)

Weekly: covers by daypart, average check, reservation vs. walk-in mix, no-show rate, beverage attach rate, Google/Resy rating, social engagement, email list growth, event leads → bookings conversion, marketing spend per incremental cover.
