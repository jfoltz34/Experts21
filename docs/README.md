## Executive summary

- **Market:** The City of Tampa has 413.5K residents (+7.3% since 2020) in a 2.5M-person metro division. Household income is polarized, and $200K+ is the single largest income bracket (about 16% of households). Tourism set records: Hillsborough had **$1.2B+ in taxable hotel revenue in FY2025**. The MICHELIN Guide (3 one-star restaurants in Tampa for 2026) has raised diners' expectations.
- **Demand:** Weekend brunch is a deeply rooted social ritual, driven by young professionals, affluent South Tampa families, visitors and celebration groups. **Weekday breakfast is weak and getting weaker.** Daily Eats closed in June 2026 citing slow weekday breakfast and GLP-1 drugs.
- **Competition:** The top of the market is crowded and capable: Oxford Exchange, On Swann, The Pearl, Boulon, Predalina, Ponte, Psomi, Market at EDITION, and others. Rome + Fig (a 2026 brunch runner-up) closed. **White space:** a daytime-only destination with a pastry engine, Florida-rooted identity, event-first design, a strong zero-proof program, and reservation-led service.
- **Startup costs (Estimate):** **≈$1.8M–$4.0M** for about 4,000 sq ft and 160 seats in a second-generation space (base ≈$2.7M including reserve and contingency). Taking over an equipped closed restaurant could bring that to ≈$1.2M–$1.8M. Add ≈$210K–$250K only if a quota liquor license must be bought.
- **Operating economics (Estimate):** Base case ≈$3.6M sales gives ≈12–13% restaurant EBITDA. **Break-even is ≈$2.0M–$2.2M/yr at EBITDA, or ≈$2.4M–$2.7M/yr after debt service.** Targets: prime cost ≤62–65%, occupancy ≤8–10%.
- **Licensing:** The DBPR food license is cheap (≈$294/yr for 150–249 seats). **Alcohol is the critical path.** Design to qualify for the **4COP-SRX** license (≥2,500 sq ft service area, ≥150 seats, ≥51% food revenue; about $1,820/yr) and secure City of Tampa AB Special Use approval. Make the lease contingent on both.
- **Staffing:** About 55–70 employees (35–45 FTE). The Florida minimum wage is now **$15.00** (tipped $11.98), inflation-indexed from 2027. E-Verify is required at 25+ employees. Daytime hours help recruiting.
- **Marketing:** Instagram/TikTok and Google are the main discovery channels. Use local media (Tampa Bay Times, Creative Loafing, TAMPA Magazine, That's So Tampa), hotel concierges and event packages. The key dates are Gasparilla, Easter, Mother's Day and Tampa Bay Restaurant Month (June).
- **Top risks:** (1) weak weekday demand, (2) soft consumer spending (a "brutal" H1 2026 locally), (3) build-out overruns (20–35% typical), (4) labor costs, (5) hurricanes, flood and insurance, (6) liquor and zoning approvals, (7) strong competition.

## Shared assumptions (the reference concept)

All cost and P&L files model the same reference concept so agents stay consistent:

| Parameter | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Format | Upscale, full-service, daytime-focused brunch, full bar |
| Hours | ~8am–3pm daily (weekday hours open to revision) |
| Size / seats | ~4,000 sq ft interior + covered patio, ~160 seats |
| Location shortlist | Hyde Park / SoHo, Water Street / Channel District, Midtown / Westshore |
| Space | Second-generation restaurant space preferred |
| Average check | ~$44 per guest (base), food + beverage, pre-tax/tip |

## Guidance for agents using this pack

1. **Treat "Estimate" figures as starting hypotheses.** Refine them with quotes, broker opinions and menu pricing studies.
2. **Treat "Verify" items as open tasks.** Confirm them with the primary agency (DBPR, City of Tampa, Hillsborough County, FL DOR) or counsel.
3. **Pay attention to dates.** Wage, fee and competitor information is current as of November 2026. Florida wages re-index every September 30 starting 2027.
4. **Cross-reference [09-risks.md](09-risks.md)** before making any recommendation. Each agent should say which risks its recommendation reduces or increases.

## Open questions for the next phase

- Final site shortlist with zoning/AB status, flood zone, rent and parking.
- Concept name, menu and price architecture (validated against competitor menus).
- Bottomless drink program: legal confirmation and effect on the SRX food-revenue ratio.
- Exact Sunday alcohol-sale start time in the City of Tampa.
- Real insurance quotes (property/wind, flood, liquor liability, business interruption).
- Contractor and equipment bids for the chosen site.
- Funding structure (SBA 7(a) vs. investor equity) and investor return targets.
