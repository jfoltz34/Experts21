## 4. How the team works together

- **Hub and spoke:** the consultant briefs **A01 Engagement Lead**, which delegates to the other agents and returns one synthesized recommendation per decision.
- **Finance check:** every proposal with a cost or revenue effect goes through **A10 FP&A** before it reaches the consultant.
- **Risk check:** every recommendation names the risks it affects (skill S02). **A26** keeps the register in [09-risks.md](09-risks.md) up to date.
- **Critical-path trio:** **A07 Real Estate**, **A13 Liquor & Zoning** and **A08 Design & Construction** work together on site selection. No lease goes forward without alcohol-approval and flood/insurance sign-off.
- **Go/no-go gates** (from [09-risks.md](09-risks.md)): Site gate (A07, A13, A14, A26), Capital gate (A10, A11), Concept gate (A04, A05, A06, A03), Team gate (A17, A18).
